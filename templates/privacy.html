Privacy Policy

Updated November 2026

1. 100% Free Service

KashFlow is a 100% free personal finance advisor and expense tracking application. We do not process payments, we do not require credit card details, and we do not sell your personal or financial data to third-party advertisers.

2. Information We Store

We store your name, email address, and a cryptographically salted password hash to authenticate your access. The transactions, budgets, bills, and goals you enter are stored in an encrypted database strictly isolated to your user ID.

3. Data Security

Your session is secured using modern cookie protection (HTTPOnly, SameSite, and HTTPS in production), form CSRF token validation, and rate-limiting on authentication endpoints to guard against brute-force attacks.

4. Data Export & Permanent Deletion

You have full sovereignty over your data. You can download all your transaction history as a CSV file at any time, or permanently delete your account and all associated financial records with one click in your Account Settings.
