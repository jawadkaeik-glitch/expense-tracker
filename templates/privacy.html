🛡️ Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026  ·  KashFlow Financial Advisor

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account on KashFlow, we collect your name and email address to manage your account and secure your data. When you log incomes, expenses, budgets, or savings goals, this financial information is stored isolated exclusively to your account.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your financial entries are used strictly to calculate your real-time Financial Health Score, compute your Daily Safe-to-Spend Allowance, generate personalized visual charts, and display actionable budget alerts.

3. Data Security & Isolation

We treat your privacy with extreme care. All passwords are cryptographically hashed using standard security algorithms (scrypt/PBKDF2). Every user's data is isolated so no third party or other user can ever view your financial records.

4. Payments & Billing

All Premium subscription transactions are processed securely through Stripe. KashFlow does not store or process your credit card numbers, CVVs, or payment details directly on our servers.

5. Data Export & Account Deletion

You retain complete ownership of your data. You can download a full CSV backup of your transactions anytime from your Account Settings, or request a complete wipe of your data.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at support@kashflow.app.
