Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Overview & 100% Free Model

KashFlow is a 100% free personal finance advisor and budget tracking application. We operate with zero monetization overhead: we do not sell products, we do not require subscription fees, we do not process payment card information, and we do not display targeted third-party advertisements.

2. Information We Collect & How It Is Used

  • Account Credentials: Your name, email address, and a cryptographically salted password hash (never stored in plain text). Used solely to authenticate your account.
  • Financial Records: Transaction descriptions, amounts, categories, dates, recurring bills, savings goals, and spending limits that you manually record. Used solely to calculate your financial health score, daily safe allowance, and budgeting progress.

* Note: KashFlow does not connect to bank APIs or scrape banking credentials. All records are entered at your discretion.

3. Zero Third-Party SDK Data Sharing

We value your privacy. KashFlow integrates NO third-party advertising SDKs, NO analytics trackers (such as Google Analytics or Facebook Pixel), and NO data brokers. Your personal and financial information is never sold, leased, or shared with third parties.

4. Data Security & Storage

All data transmitted between your device and our servers is protected using TLS/HTTPS encryption with HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS). Sessions utilize hardened HTTPOnly, SameSite cookie flags, and form CSRF token verification. Backend databases enforce strict tenant isolation ensuring users can only query their own records.

5. Data Retention, Portability & Deletion

Your records are retained only as long as your account remains active. You maintain complete sovereignty over your data:

  • Data Export: Download all income and expense transactions in a standard CSV file anytime via Settings.
  • Data Restore: Re-import your transactions from a CSV backup without creating duplicate records.
  • Account Deletion: Permanently delete your account and all associated records directly from Settings or via our public Account Deletion Request Portal. Deletions are irreversible and execute a hard database purge.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request data assistance, contact our privacy officer at support@kashflow.app.
